About Bangda Technology

Bangda Technology is a professional manufacturer specializing in custom sheet metal fabrication and CNC machining services. With years of experience in the industry, we are committed to providing high-quality metal parts for industrial, automotive, and electronic applications.

Our Strengths

  • Advanced production equipment: We have a full set of CNC machining centers, sheet metal bending machines, punching machines, and surface treatment equipment to ensure product precision and quality.
  • Professional team: Our engineers and technicians have rich experience in product design, processing, and quality control, providing one-stop solutions for customers.
  • Strict quality control: We implement strict quality inspection standards from raw materials to finished products, ensuring each product meets customer requirements.
  • OEM/ODM service: We support custom design and production according to customer drawings and samples, from prototype to mass production.

Our Services

  • Sheet metal enclosures and cabinets
  • Precision CNC machined parts (aluminum, steel, stainless steel)
  • Surface treatment: powder coating, anodizing, polishing
  • Custom OEM/ODM solutions
